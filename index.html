📖 How to Play

1Collect elixir ⚡
Elixir refills automatically over time. Every card costs elixir, so spend it wisely.

2Deploy troops 🃏
Tap a card, then tap your half of the arena. Troops march toward the enemy towers.

3Cast spells 🔥
Fireball and Freeze can be cast anywhere on the arena. Use them to clear swarms.

4Destroy towers 🏰
Break the side towers to open a path, then finish the King Tower for an instant win.

5Cross the bridges 🌉
Troops can only cross the river using the two bridges. Defend your lanes.

6Control territory 🗺️
Every tower you keep controls territory. Push the line toward the enemy King.

7Double elixir ⏱
At 1:00 remaining elixir flows twice as fast. Overtime decides a tie.

8Earn trophies 🏆
Win battles to climb arenas, collect coins and upgrade your cards.
